En life-la nee oru romba romba favorite-aana person.

Enaku epo oru confused state vandhalum, illa naan edhavadhu thappa yosikren nu thonunaalum, naan odane unkita dhaan வந்து keppen. Nee tharra andha solution eppovume thappa ponadhe illa. Adhu eppadi nu enaku innum therila!

Life-la edhavadhu problem illa romba sad-aa irukumbodhu, unkita sonna odane adhu automatically resolve aana madhuri oru feel kedaikum. Unkita pesumbodhu kedaikura andha relief vere engayume kedaikadhu.

Enaku udambu sari illana, illa edhavadhu hurt aayiduchu-na... nee adha udane notice panni, adikkadi 'eppadi iruku' nu kepa. Sila per idha kepanga, neraya per keka maatanga, aana nee enaku avlo importance kuduthu enna pathi yosikuradhu enaku romba romba pudichiruku. Adhu enna romba special-aa feel panna vekkudhu.

Namma bond evlo special nu unake theriyum. Indha friendship innum romba naalaiku, apdiye life long namma koodave varanum nu naan romba aasaipadren.

Once again Happy Birthday! Epovume ippadiye anba, enna care panra oru nalla best friend-aa iru! ❤️
